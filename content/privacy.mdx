# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: May 1, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Willpwr ("we," "us," or "our") collects,
uses, and protects your personal data when you use the Willpwr website at
getwillpwr.com or the Willpwr application at willpwr.app.

## 1. Categories of personal data we collect

- **Account data:** name, email address, password hash, account preferences.
- **Usage data:** tasks, notes, AI conversations, focus session history,
  calendar events you've connected (read-only or read/write per your
  authorization).
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages
  viewed, referring URL, cookie identifiers.
- **Payment data:** name, billing address, and last 4 digits of card. Full
  card numbers are stored only by our payment processor and never reach our
  servers.

## 2. Purposes of data collection and processing

- Provide and operate the Willpwr service.
- Authenticate users and prevent fraud.
- Generate AI responses tailored to your work context.
- Send transactional and (with consent) marketing emails.
- Measure aggregate product usage to improve the service.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Data retention

- Account data is retained while your account is active and for 90 days after
  deletion to allow account recovery.
- Usage data is retained for 24 months and then aggregated or deleted.
- Technical data is retained for 13 months.
- Payment records are retained for 7 years to comply with tax law.

## 4. GDPR rights (EU/EEA residents)

You have the right to: access your personal data; rectify inaccuracies;
request erasure; restrict or object to processing; receive your data in a
portable format; and lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.
Email privacy@getwillpwr.com to exercise any of these rights.

## 5. CCPA rights (California residents)

You have the right to: know what personal information we collect, use,
disclose, or sell; delete personal information; and opt out of the sale of
personal information. We do not sell your personal information. Email
privacy@getwillpwr.com to exercise these rights.

## 6. Cookies and tracking

We use a small number of essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember
your preferences. With your consent, we also load PostHog for analytics. You
can manage your cookie preferences at any time from the cookie banner.

## 7. Third-party processors

We use a limited set of vendors to operate the service: Vercel (hosting),
PostHog (analytics, EU-hosted), Resend (transactional email), and Stripe
(payments). Each processor receives only the data needed to perform its role.

## 8. Contact

Email privacy@getwillpwr.com or visit [Contact](/contact) for privacy-related
questions.

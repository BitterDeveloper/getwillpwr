# Updating your payment method

You can update your card at any time from Settings → Billing.

1. Open Settings → Billing.
2. Click **Payment methods**.
3. Add a new card or remove an existing one.
4. Set the default card you'd like used for renewal.

## A payment failed — what now?

We retry failed payments three times over five days. If all three fail, your
account drops to the Free tier and your data is preserved. Update your card
and click **Reactivate** to restore your previous plan.

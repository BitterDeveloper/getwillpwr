# Managing your subscription

You can change your plan at any time from your account settings.

## Upgrade or downgrade

1. Open Settings → Billing.
2. Click **Change plan**.
3. Choose your new tier and confirm.

Upgrades take effect immediately and you're prorated. Downgrades take effect
at the end of your current billing period.

## Cancel

Open Settings → Billing → **Cancel subscription**. You'll keep access through
the end of your current period; after that, your account drops to the Free
tier. Your data stays intact.

## Refunds

If you're unsatisfied within 14 days of your first payment, [contact support](/contact)
and we'll sort it out.

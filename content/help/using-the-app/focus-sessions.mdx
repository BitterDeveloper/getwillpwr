# Running focus sessions

A focus session blocks distractions, pins one task, and tracks the time you
spend on deep work.

## Start a session

1. Pick a task from your task list.
2. Click **Focus** on the task card.
3. Choose a duration (15, 25, 50, or 90 minutes).
4. Notifications are muted automatically until the timer ends.

## Review your history

Open Insights → Deep work to see your weekly focus time, longest streaks, and
which tasks you've spent the most time on.

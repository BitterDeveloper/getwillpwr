# Asking the AI assistant

The assistant has access to your tasks, calendar, recent emails, and 90-day
goals. It uses that context to give answers that are actually relevant to
your work.

## Useful prompts

- *"What should I work on right now?"*
- *"Plan my week — I want to ship the v2 landing page by Friday."*
- *"Break this goal into weekly milestones: launch the podcast in 90 days."*
- *"Draft a reply to the email from Maria about the Q3 budget."*

## What counts as a message?

Each prompt you send to the assistant uses one message from your monthly
quota. Loading pages, viewing data, and starting focus sessions don't count.

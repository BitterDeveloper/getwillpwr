# Welcome to Willpwr

Willpwr brings your tasks, email, calendar, and focus sessions into a single
unified workspace, with an AI assistant that has full context.

## Your first 5 minutes

1. Connect a calendar (Google or Microsoft) so the AI can see your real schedule.
2. Add three tasks for today — anything counts.
3. Ask the AI: *"What should I work on first?"*
4. Start a focus session on the top suggestion.

## What the dashboard shows

The dashboard pulls together today's tasks, your next three calendar events,
and a summary panel from the AI. Everything updates in real time as you check
items off or finish a focus block.

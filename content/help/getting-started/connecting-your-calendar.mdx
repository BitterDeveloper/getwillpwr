# Connecting your calendar

Willpwr supports Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook. The AI assistant uses
your calendar to suggest when to schedule deep work, plan your week, and avoid
double-booking yourself.

## Connect Google Calendar

1. Open Settings → Integrations.
2. Click **Connect Google**.
3. Sign in with the Google account whose calendar you want to use.
4. Approve the read/write permissions.

## Connect Microsoft Outlook

1. Open Settings → Integrations.
2. Click **Connect Microsoft**.
3. Sign in with your Microsoft account.
4. Approve the requested permissions.

## What happens to my calendar data?

Calendar events are read directly each time the AI needs them. We do not store
the contents of meetings. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for full details.
